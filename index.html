2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide to us

  • Contact details (such as your email address) when you contact us for support.
  • Account identifiers for social media accounts you authorize us to manage.

2.2 Information we receive from Meta platforms

When you connect a Facebook Page, Instagram Business/Creator account, or Threads account to our application and grant permissions, we may receive:

  • The account’s public profile information (account ID, username, display name);
  • Page or business identifiers and the association between them;
  • Access tokens issued by Meta authorizing specific API actions;
  • Content you ask us to publish (text, images, video) and its resulting post/media IDs;
  • Basic engagement metrics for content published through our tools (for example, like, comment, and view counts made available by the platform API).

We do not collect passwords. Authentication to Meta platforms occurs through Meta’s own OAuth authorization flow, and we receive only tokens — never your Meta password.

2.3 Information collected automatically

  • Operational logs generated by our own publishing pipeline (timestamps, job status, error messages) used for reliability and debugging.
  • Basic, non-identifying technical information on our website such as the page you requested, used for serving the page correctly.

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these cases:

  • With the platforms you connect (Meta, X, YouTube, TikTok) — to the extent necessary to publish content and read metrics you requested, as governed by their own privacy policies.
  • With service providers — infrastructure, storage, and AI model providers that process data strictly on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
  • For legal reasons — where required by law, subpoena, or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of our users or the public.
  • In a business transfer — if our operation is merged or acquired, information may transfer as part of that transaction, with this policy continuing to apply.

6. Data Retention

  • Access tokens are retained only while your account remains connected; deleting the connection invalidates and removes the stored token.
  • Operational logs are retained for a limited period (typically up to 90 days) and then deleted or anonymized.
  • Published content and post metadata are retained as long as needed to provide the service and to maintain publishing history.
  • Support correspondence is retained as long as necessary to resolve your request and meet record-keeping obligations.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including restricting access to credentials, storing tokens with restrictive file permissions on encrypted storage, using HTTPS for all transfers, and rotating secrets when compromise is suspected. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International Data Transfers

Our systems and service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers, including standard contractual clauses.
